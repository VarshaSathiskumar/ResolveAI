# Brewwell Brew Pro 200 Limited Warranty

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Warranty term

The Brew Pro 200 is covered by a 24 month limited warranty from the date of purchase. Keep your receipt as proof of purchase.

## What is covered

Defects in materials and workmanship of the pump, heating element, control board and piercing needle assembly under normal household use.

## What is not covered

- Damage from limescale when the machine was not descaled as described in the manual.
- Cosmetic damage, drops, spills and misuse.
- Commercial or shared office use.
- Consumables such as the drip tray, used-pod bin and needle cleaning tool.

## Making a claim

Contact Brewwell support with your model, purchase date and a description of the problem, including the steps already tried. Support will open a case and arrange a repair or replacement for covered faults.

# Brewwell Brew Pro 200 Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Machine powers on but no coffee comes out, only a few drops | Clogged piercing needle (E01) | Clogged needle |
| Amber light blinking, flow is slow | Limescale (E03) | Descale required |
| Steady red light | Tank empty or not seated | Tank not detected |
| Coffee is weak or lukewarm | Old pod, short cup setting, scale | Weak or cold coffee |
| Water leaks under the machine | Pod lid not latched or tray overflow | Leaks |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Clogged needle (error E01)

The red light blinks and the machine will not brew, or only a few drops come out of the outlet while the pump sounds normal. This usually means the piercing needle is blocked by coffee grounds.

1. Unplug the machine and let it cool for five minutes.
2. Open the lid and look at the small hollow needle at the top of the pod chamber. If its opening is packed with grounds, it is blocked.
3. Use the supplied needle cleaning tool, or a straightened paper clip, to clear the opening. Never use a sharp knife.
4. Rinse the pod chamber under running water and let it dry.
5. Plug in, run a rinse cycle (hold the power button for 3 seconds) and try a pod again.

If the needle is bent or the red light still blinks after cleaning, the needle assembly needs service. It is covered by the warranty.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Descale required (error E03)

The amber light blinks, brew volume drops and the machine sounds strained. Limescale is restricting the water path.

Follow the descaling procedure in the user manual, page 7. Run the descale solution through the machine, then two fresh water rinses. If the amber light keeps blinking after a full descale, hold the power button for 10 seconds to reset the counter.

## Tank not detected

A steady red light means the tank is empty or not fully seated. Fill the tank to the MAX line and push it onto the back of the machine until it clicks.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Weak or cold coffee

Use a fresh pod, select the standard cup setting rather than the short cup, and preheat by running a rinse cycle first. Descale if it has been more than 3 months.

## Leaks

Check that the handle is fully latched. Empty the drip tray. If water pools under the machine while idle, unplug it and contact support.

## When to contact support

If the machine does not recover after the steps above, contact Brewwell support with the model, the error light pattern and the steps you tried.

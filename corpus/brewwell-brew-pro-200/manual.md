# Brewwell Brew Pro 200 User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome and safety

Thank you for choosing the Brewwell Brew Pro 200 single-serve pod coffee machine. Read this manual before first use and keep it for reference.

- Use only on a stable, level surface away from the edge of the counter.
- Never open the pod lid while a brew is in progress. The brew chamber is hot and under pressure.
- Unplug the machine before cleaning the piercing needle or the pod chamber.
- Do not immerse the machine, cord or plug in water.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Box contents and parts

The box contains the machine, a 1.0 litre removable water tank, a drip tray with grille, a used-pod bin, a needle cleaning tool and this manual.

Main parts: pod lid and handle, pod chamber, piercing needle (the small hollow needle at the top of the pod chamber that pierces the pod and lets hot water in), water tank, drip tray, power button.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Controls and indicator lights

The Brew Pro 200 has one power button and three indicator lights.

| Light | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Steady white | Ready to brew |
| Pulsing white | Heating up |
| Blinking amber | Descale required (error E03) |
| Blinking red | Brew blocked (error E01) |
| Steady red | Water tank empty or not seated |

Press the power button once to brew a standard 200 ml cup. Press twice for a 120 ml short cup. Hold for 3 seconds to start a rinse cycle.

## First-time setup

1. Rinse the water tank and fill it with fresh cold water to the MAX line.
2. Slide the tank onto the back of the machine until it clicks.
3. Place the drip tray and the used-pod bin.
4. Plug in and press the power button. The light pulses white while the machine heats.
5. When the light is steady white, hold the power button for 3 seconds with no pod inserted to run the first rinse cycle into a mug. Discard the water.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## Brewing a cup

1. Lift the handle and insert one Brewwell-compatible pod, foil side up.
2. Close the handle firmly. You should feel it latch.
3. Place a cup under the outlet.
4. Press the power button once for a standard cup or twice for a short cup.
5. When brewing stops, wait ten seconds, then lift the handle. The used pod drops into the bin.

Use fresh cold water for every brew. Do not use pods that are damaged or already pierced.

## Cleaning and care

Wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Wash the drip tray, grille and used-pod bin weekly in warm soapy water.

### Cleaning the piercing needle

Coffee grounds can build up around the piercing needle over time. Once a month, unplug the machine, open the lid and use the supplied needle cleaning tool to clear the needle opening. Rinse the pod chamber under running water and let it dry before use.

<!-- page: 7 -->
## Descaling

Limescale reduces flow and can damage the pump and heating element. Descale every 3 months, or immediately when the light blinks amber (E03).

1. Empty the tank and fill it with Brewwell descaling solution mixed with water as directed on the pack.
2. Place a large container under the outlet. Do not insert a pod.
3. Hold the power button for 3 seconds to start the rinse cycle, then repeat until the tank is empty.
4. Rinse the tank, fill it with fresh water and run two more rinse cycles.
5. The amber light stops blinking when descaling is complete.

Keep the descaling date. Warranty service may ask for proof that the machine has been descaled regularly.

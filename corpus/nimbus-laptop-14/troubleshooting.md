# Nimbus Air 14 Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop is slow, apps take long to open | Full drive or too many startup apps | Laptop is slow |
| Battery drains quickly | Background apps, bright screen | Battery drains quickly |
| Fan is loud and the base is hot | Blocked vents or heavy load | Fan is loud |
| Laptop will not turn on | Flat battery or charger fault | Will not turn on |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Laptop is slow

The laptop is slow to start, apps take a long time to open or the pointer stutters. A full drive, many startup apps or a pending update is the usual cause.

1. Restart the laptop, because a restart clears memory that apps have not released.
2. Open Settings, Storage and free at least 15 percent of the drive by removing unused apps and large files.
3. Open Startup apps and turn off the ones you do not need at sign-in.
4. Install the latest system update and restart again.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Battery drains quickly

The battery empties much faster than before.

1. Open Settings, Battery usage and look for an app with high background use.
2. Lower the screen brightness and turn on Battery Saver.
3. Unplug USB devices you are not using.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Fan is loud

The fan runs loudly and the base of the laptop is hot.

1. Put the laptop on a hard, flat surface so the vents are free.
2. Close heavy apps such as video editors and games.
3. Clean the vents with a soft brush or a short burst of compressed air.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## Will not turn on

The screen stays black when you press the power button.

1. Connect the supplied charger and wait 15 minutes.
2. Hold the power button for 10 seconds, release it and press it once.
3. Try a different wall socket and cable.

If it still does not start, contact support with the steps you tried.

<!-- page: 6 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

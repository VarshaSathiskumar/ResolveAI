# Nimbus Air 14 User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome and safety

Thank you for choosing the Nimbus Air 14 laptop. Use only the supplied 65 W USB-C charger. Do not use the laptop on a bed or sofa, where the vents can be blocked.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Ports and keys

The Air 14 has two USB-C ports, one USB-A port and a headphone jack. The power button is at the top right of the keyboard. Holding it for 10 seconds forces a shutdown.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Storage and memory

The laptop has 16 GB of memory and a 512 GB solid state drive. Keep at least 15 percent of the drive free so the system can work smoothly.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Battery care

Battery Saver mode extends battery life by lowering brightness and background activity. Battery usage in Settings shows which apps used the most power.

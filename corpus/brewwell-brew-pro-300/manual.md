# Brewwell Brew Pro 300 User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome and safety

Thank you for choosing the Brewwell Brew Pro 300 pod coffee machine with built-in milk frother. Read this manual before first use.

- Use only on a stable, level surface.
- The steam wand and frother jug become hot. Handle by the grip only.
- Unplug before cleaning. Do not immerse the machine in water.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Box contents and parts

The box contains the machine, a 1.4 litre removable water tank, a tank seal ring (a small black silicone ring fitted around the tank outlet valve), a drip tray, a used-pod bin, a frother jug and this manual.

The tank seal ring keeps the connection between the tank and the pump airtight. Do not remove it when washing the tank without refitting it.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Display and controls

The colour display shows the machine status and error codes. Use the dial to choose a drink and press it to start.

| Display message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ready | Machine is heated and ready |
| E03 Descale | Descaling is required |
| E04 Tank seal | The tank seal ring is missing or not seated, the pump cannot draw water |
| Fill tank | Tank is empty |

<!-- page: 4 -->
## First-time setup

1. Check that the black tank seal ring is fitted around the outlet valve at the bottom of the tank.
2. Fill the tank with fresh cold water to the MAX line and push it onto the machine until it clicks.
3. Plug in and press the power key. Wait for Ready on the display.
4. Run the first rinse: open the menu, choose Rinse and confirm with the dial.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## Brewing and milk drinks

1. Insert a pod and close the handle until it latches.
2. Choose Espresso, Lungo or Coffee on the dial and press to start.
3. For milk drinks, fill the frother jug to the line, attach it to the frother port and choose Latte or Cappuccino.

<!-- page: 6 -->
## Cleaning

Rinse the frother jug after every milk drink. Weekly, wash the drip tray, used-pod bin and tank in warm soapy water.

### Washing the tank and seal ring

Pull the black seal ring off the outlet valve, wash it and the valve, then press the ring back on until it sits flat in its groove. A missing or twisted ring causes error E04.

<!-- page: 7 -->
## Descaling

Descale every 3 months or when the display shows E03. Open the menu, choose Descale and follow the on-screen steps with Brewwell descaling solution. The cycle takes about 20 minutes.

# Brewwell Brew Pro 300 Limited Warranty

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Warranty term

The Brew Pro 300 is covered by a 24 month limited warranty from the date of purchase. Keep your receipt as proof of purchase.

## What is covered

Defects in materials and workmanship of the pump, heating element, control board, milk frother motor and display under normal household use.

## What is not covered

- Lost or damaged tank seal ring, drip tray, used-pod bin and frother jug.
- Damage from limescale when the machine was not descaled as described in the manual.
- Cosmetic damage, drops, spills and misuse.
- Commercial use.

## Making a claim

Contact Brewwell support with your model, purchase date and the steps already tried. Support will open a case and arrange repair or replacement for covered faults.

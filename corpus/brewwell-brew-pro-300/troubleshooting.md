# Brewwell Brew Pro 300 Troubleshooting Guide

## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Pump runs but no water or coffee comes out | Tank seal ring missing or misseated (E04) | Tank seal error |
| Display shows E03 or flow is slow | Limescale | Descale required |
| Milk is not frothing | Frother jug blocked or not attached | Frother problems |
| Machine is very loud while brewing | Air in the pump, often from the tank seal | Tank seal error |

## Tank seal error (E04)

The display shows E04 Tank seal. The pump is noisy and runs without drawing any water, so nothing comes out of the outlet and the machine will not brew. The tank seal ring is usually missing, twisted or not seated, which lets air into the pump line.

1. Switch the machine off and lift the tank away.
2. Look at the outlet valve at the bottom of the tank. A black silicone ring should sit flat in the groove around it.
3. If the ring is missing, look in the sink or tank basin. If it is damaged or cannot be found, order a replacement seal ring from Brewwell support.
4. If the ring is twisted, remove it and press it back so it sits flat all the way round.
5. Refill the tank, push it firmly on until it clicks, then run a rinse from the menu. The error clears when water flows.

A lost or damaged seal ring is a consumable and is not covered by the warranty. The pump itself is covered if it is damaged by running dry.

## Descale required (error E03)

Run the Descale program from the menu as described in the user manual, page 7. If E03 stays after a full cycle, hold the dial for 10 seconds to reset the counter.

## Frother problems

Detach the jug lid, rinse the frother whisk and the milk path under warm water, then reassemble. Use cold milk.

## When to contact support

If the machine still does not draw water after fitting a seal ring and rinsing, contact Brewwell support with the model and the error code shown.

# Brewwell DripMate 12 Limited Warranty

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Warranty term

The DripMate 12 is covered by a 12 month limited warranty from the date of purchase.

## What is covered

Defects in materials and workmanship of the heating plate, control board and thermal fuse under normal household use.

## What is not covered

- Glass carafe breakage.
- Damage from limescale.
- Cosmetic damage and misuse.
- Commercial use.

## Out of warranty

After 12 months, Brewwell support can still log a case and quote a paid repair or recommend a replacement model.

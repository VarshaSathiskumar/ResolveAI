# Brewwell DripMate 12 Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Machine is on but brews very slowly or only half a pot | Limescale in the water path | Slow or partial brew |
| Water overflows the basket | Blocked showerhead or too fine a grind | Overflow |
| No power, no lights | Thermal fuse tripped | No power |
| Coffee tastes bitter or sour | Old coffee, wrong grind, scale | Taste |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Slow or partial brew

If the DripMate 12 is not brewing a full pot, drips slowly or stops partway, limescale is the most common cause. Descale following the user manual, page 5, using white vinegar. Then run two pots of fresh water. Check the showerhead holes for grounds as well.

## Overflow

Check the showerhead holes and the basket for blockages. Use a medium grind, not a fine one, and do not fill the basket above the line.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## No power

If nothing lights up, check the outlet with another appliance. Unplug the DripMate for 30 minutes and plug it back in. If the clock still stays dark the thermal fuse has tripped and the machine needs service, which is covered only while the warranty is valid.

## When to contact support

Contact Brewwell support with the model, purchase date and the steps already tried.

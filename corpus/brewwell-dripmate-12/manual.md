# Brewwell DripMate 12 User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome and safety

The Brewwell DripMate 12 makes up to 12 cups of filter coffee. Place it on a flat surface with space above for the lid to open. The warming plate stays hot after brewing. Unplug before cleaning.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Parts

Water reservoir with window and cup markings, showerhead (the perforated disc under the lid that spreads water over the grounds), basket holder, permanent filter basket, glass carafe, warming plate, clock and brew button.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Brewing

1. Fill the reservoir with cold water to the number of cups you want.
2. Place a paper filter or the permanent basket in the basket holder and add one level tablespoon of ground coffee per cup.
3. Close the lid, place the carafe on the warming plate and press Brew.
4. Brewing takes about 8 minutes for a full pot. The machine keeps coffee warm for 120 minutes and then switches off.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Cleaning

Wash the carafe, basket and basket holder after each use. Wipe the showerhead with a damp cloth and check that none of its holes are blocked by grounds.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## Descaling

Descale monthly in hard water areas, otherwise every 3 months. Fill the reservoir to the 12 cup mark with equal parts white vinegar and water, brew half the pot, switch off for 30 minutes, then brew the rest. Run two full pots of fresh water to rinse.

# Shelter Travel Umbrella Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Does not open or close | Stuck runner | Does not open |
| Bent rib | Wind | Bent rib |
| Canopy tears | Wind or snagging | Torn canopy |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Does not open

The button is pressed but the canopy stays shut or jams half way.

1. Open the umbrella as far as it goes and wipe the shaft with a dry cloth.
2. Add a drop of light oil to the runner and work it up and down.
3. Check that no rib is caught under the canopy.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Bent rib

A rib is bent after wind.

1. Open the umbrella and bend the rib gently back into line with both hands.
2. If it snaps, the frame needs service.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Torn canopy

The canopy has a tear.

1. Small tears can be closed with a fabric patch on the inside.
2. A large tear is not covered by the warranty.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

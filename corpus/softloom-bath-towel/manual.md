# Softloom Bath Towel User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome

Thank you for choosing the Softloom Bath Towel, made from 550 gsm combed cotton. Wash it once before first use.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Washing

Wash at 40 degrees with similar colours. Do not use bleach. Tumble dry on low heat or hang to dry.

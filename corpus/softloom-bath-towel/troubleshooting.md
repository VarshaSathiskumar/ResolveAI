# Softloom Bath Towel Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Towel feels stiff or does not absorb | Fabric softener build-up | Not absorbent |
| Lint on skin and in the machine | New cotton fibres | Lint |
| Colours fade | Hot wash or sunlight | Fading |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Not absorbent

Water beads on the surface instead of soaking in. Fabric softener leaves a coating on cotton.

1. Wash the towel on its own with one cup of white vinegar and no detergent.
2. Run a second rinse with plain water.
3. Stop using fabric softener on towels.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Lint

Loose fibres come off in the first washes.

1. Wash the towel separately from dark clothes.
2. Shake it out before drying and clean the dryer filter after each load.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Fading

The colour looks washed out.

1. Wash at 30 degrees inside out.
2. Dry in the shade rather than in direct sun.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

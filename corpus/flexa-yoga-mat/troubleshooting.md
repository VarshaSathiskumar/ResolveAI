# Flexa Yoga Mat 6mm Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Mat slips on the floor | Dust or oil on the underside | Mat slips |
| Top layer peels | Heat or sun | Peeling |
| Strong rubber smell | New rubber | Rubber smell |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Mat slips

The mat moves on the floor or feels slippery to the hands.

1. Wipe both sides with a cloth dampened with water and a little vinegar.
2. Let it dry fully before use.
3. Practise on a clean, dry floor.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Peeling

The top layer lifts at the edges.

1. Keep the mat away from heat and sun.
2. Contact support if the layer peels in normal use.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Rubber smell

A new mat smells of rubber.

1. Air it unrolled in a ventilated room for a day.
2. Wipe it with a damp cloth.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

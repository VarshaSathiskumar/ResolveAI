# TrailPack Daypack 22L Limited Warranty

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Warranty term

The Daypack 22L is covered by a 36 month limited warranty from the date of purchase. Keep your receipt as proof of purchase.

## What is covered

Defects in materials and workmanship of the zips, straps, seams, buckles under normal household use.

## What is not covered

- Overloading beyond 10 kg.
- Cuts and burns.
- Cosmetic wear.

## Making a claim

Contact support with your model, purchase date and a description of the problem, including the steps already tried. Support will open a case and arrange a repair or replacement for covered faults.

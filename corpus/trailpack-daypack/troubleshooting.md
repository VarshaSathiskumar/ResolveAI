# TrailPack Daypack 22L Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Zip is stuck | Fabric caught in teeth | Stuck zip |
| Strap tears | Overloading | Strap tear |
| Water gets in | Worn coating | Water getting in |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Stuck zip

The zip will not move or splits behind the slider.

1. Free any fabric caught in the teeth without pulling the slider.
2. Rub a candle or a bar of soap along the teeth.
3. Squeeze the slider gently with pliers if it has opened up.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Strap tear

A strap seam is coming away.

1. Stop carrying heavy loads.
2. Stitch it with strong thread, or contact support if it is a covered seam.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Water getting in

The bag is damp inside after rain.

1. Dry the bag open, then spray it with a fabric waterproofing spray.
2. Use the rain cover stored in the bottom pocket.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

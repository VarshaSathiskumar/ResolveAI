# Google Pixel 9 Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Start here: the phone is not working properly

A general complaint such as "my phone is acting up" or "something is wrong" needs narrowing down before a fix is suggested. Most Pixel 9 complaints fall into six areas: charging, battery life, heat, speed, signal and the screen.

- Ask: Is the problem with charging, battery life, heat, speed, signal or the screen?
- Ask: Did it start after a software update, a drop or water getting in?
- Ask: Do you see a message on the screen?

| Area | Typical complaint | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Charging | Does not charge, charges slowly, "Charging paused" | Charging problems |
| Battery life | Battery drains quickly | Battery drains quickly |
| Heat | Phone gets hot | Phone runs hot |
| Speed | Slow, laggy or freezing | Slow or freezing |
| Signal | No mobile data, no bars, Wi-Fi drops | Signal and data problems |
| Screen | Touch ignored, flicker, dim | Screen problems |

## Wired charging does not start

The phone does not react when the cable is plugged in, or the charging icon never appears.

1. Try a different USB-C cable and a different USB-PD charger or wall socket.
2. Switch the phone off and look into the USB-C port with a torch. Remove lint with a wooden toothpick, never metal.
3. Restart the phone and plug the cable in again.
4. Charge the phone for 30 minutes with the screen off before you turn it on.

If the port is loose or bent, the charging port needs service. It is covered by the warranty.

## Wireless charging does not start

The phone does not charge on the pad, or charging stops and starts.

1. Centre the phone on the pad and remove any case thicker than 3 mm, or any case with a metal plate or magnet.
2. Check that the pad is plugged into a USB-PD charger, not a computer port.
3. Try a different wireless pad.

## Charging paused or slow because of heat

The screen says "Charging paused" and charging is slow. The phone protects the battery when it is hot.

1. Unplug the phone and take it out of its case and out of direct sunlight.
2. Close apps that run in the background, such as navigation, games and video calls.
3. Let the phone cool for 10 minutes, then plug it in again on a hard surface, not a bed or a sofa.

## Battery drains quickly

A battery that loses its charge much faster than before is usually caused by one app, a weak signal or screen settings.

1. Open Settings, Battery, Battery usage and see which app used the most power.
2. Turn on Adaptive Battery and Battery Saver.
3. Restart the phone and watch the battery for a day.

## Battery drains with the screen off

The battery falls overnight or while the phone sits idle.

1. Open Settings, Battery, Battery usage and look for an app with unusually high background use.
2. Restrict background activity for that app, or uninstall it.
3. Turn on Adaptive Battery and Battery Saver.
4. Restart the phone to clear stuck background processes.

## Battery drains while in use

The battery falls quickly during normal use.

1. Lower the screen brightness and turn on Adaptive brightness.
2. Turn off always-on display and location for apps that do not need it.
3. Check the signal bars: a weak signal makes the phone use more power. Switch to Wi-Fi where you can.

## Phone runs hot

The back of the phone is hot to the touch, even with light use.

1. Remove the case and stop charging for a while.
2. Close apps running in the background and stop navigation, games or video calls.
3. Update the apps and the system, because a stuck app is a common cause.
4. If the phone is still hot with nothing running, restart it, and contact support if it stays hot.

## Slow or freezing

The phone lags, apps close by themselves or the screen freezes.

1. Restart the phone.
2. Free at least 3 GB of storage by removing unused apps and photos.
3. Install the latest system update.

## Slow in one app

1. Force stop the app, then clear its cache in Settings, Apps.
2. Update the app, and if it still lags, uninstall it and install it again.

## Slow in everything

1. Hold the power button for 30 seconds to force a restart.
2. Free at least 3 GB of storage by removing unused apps and photos.
3. Install the latest system update.
4. Start the phone in safe mode to check whether a downloaded app is the cause.

## Signal and data problems

The phone shows no bars, mobile data does not work or Wi-Fi keeps dropping.

1. Switch airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off.
2. Restart the phone.
3. Check the signal in another room or place, to tell a network fault from a phone fault.

## Mobile data does not work

1. Switch airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off.
2. Remove the SIM and put it back in, or switch the eSIM off and on.
3. Check that mobile data is on and the data limit is not reached, in Settings, Network.
4. Reset the network settings and restart the phone.

## Wi-Fi keeps dropping

1. Forget the network and join it again.
2. Restart the router and the phone.
3. Move closer to the router and away from thick walls.

## Screen problems

The screen ignores touch, flickers or is too dim.

1. Remove the screen protector and clean the screen with a dry cloth.
2. Restart the phone. Hold the power button for 30 seconds if the screen does not respond.
3. Lower the refresh rate in Settings, Display, and check that Adaptive brightness is on.
4. A cracked screen or dead pixels need service. Screen damage from a drop is not covered by the warranty.

## When to contact support

If the phone does not recover after the steps above, contact support with the model, the area of the problem and the steps you tried.

# Google Pixel 9 User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome and safety

Thank you for choosing the Pixel 9 smartphone. Read this manual before first use.

- Use only a USB-C cable and a charger that carries the USB-PD certification mark.
- Do not charge the phone on a bed, a sofa or under a pillow, where heat cannot escape.
- Do not use a phone with a swollen battery or a cracked back. Stop using it and contact support.
- The phone is water resistant, not waterproof. Dry the USB-C port before you connect a cable.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Buttons and ports

The power button is on the right edge, above the volume buttons. The USB-C port and the SIM tray are on the bottom edge. A press of the power button for 30 seconds forces a restart when the screen is frozen.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Charging

The Pixel 9 charges at up to 27 W with a USB-PD charger and at up to 12 W on a Qi wireless charger. Charging pauses at high temperature to protect the battery, and the screen shows "Charging paused". It resumes by itself when the phone cools.

Wireless charging works best when the phone sits in the centre of the pad and the case is thinner than 3 mm with no metal plates or magnets.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Battery care

Adaptive Battery learns which apps you rarely use and limits their background activity. Battery Saver extends battery life by reducing performance and background sync. In Settings, Battery shows which apps used the most power since the last full charge.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## Network and connections

The Pixel 9 supports Wi-Fi 7, 5G and 4G. It takes one nano-SIM and one eSIM. Airplane mode switches off all radios. If mobile data stops after a network change, toggling airplane mode for 10 seconds makes the phone register on the network again.

<!-- page: 6 -->
## Software updates

Updates download over Wi-Fi and install overnight while the phone charges. Keep at least 3 GB of storage free. Restart the phone once a week to clear background processes.

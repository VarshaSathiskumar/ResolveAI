# Lumio Desk Lamp D2 Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Lamp does not turn on | Adapter not seated | Does not turn on |
| Light flickers | Loose plug or dimmer setting | Flickers |
| Dial ignores touch | Dirty dial | Dial ignores touch |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Does not turn on

The lamp stays dark when you touch the dial.

1. Check that the adapter is fully pushed into the base and the wall socket.
2. Try another wall socket.
3. Unplug for 30 seconds and plug in again.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Flickers

The light flickers or buzzes at low brightness.

1. Turn the brightness up to the middle.
2. Push the plug into the base until it clicks.
3. Try another wall socket away from large appliances.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Dial ignores touch

The dial does not respond.

1. Unplug the lamp and wipe the dial with a dry cloth.
2. Plug in again and touch the dial with a dry fingertip.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

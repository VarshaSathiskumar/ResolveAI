# Lumio Desk Lamp D2 User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome and safety

Thank you for choosing the Lumio Desk Lamp D2. Use only the supplied 12 V adapter. Do not cover the lamp head while it is on.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Controls

Touch the dial once to switch on or off. Turn it to change brightness. Hold it for 2 seconds to change the colour temperature.

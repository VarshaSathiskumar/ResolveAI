# Stackwell Clear Box 30L Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Lid does not latch | Bent clip or overfilled box | Lid does not latch |
| Cracked corner | Impact or overloading | Cracks |
| Cloudy plastic | Sunlight and harsh cleaners | Cloudy plastic |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Lid does not latch

The lid sits open or pops off when the box is lifted.

1. Remove items above the rim so the lid can sit flat.
2. Check that all four clips are in their slots and press each one until it clicks.
3. If a clip is bent, warm it in hot water for a minute and press it gently back into shape.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Cracks

A crack near a corner or the base can spread under load.

1. Empty the box and stop stacking others on it.
2. Use it only for light items, or replace the box if the crack goes through the wall.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Cloudy plastic

The clear plastic has turned cloudy.

1. Wash with warm soapy water and rinse well.
2. Wipe with a cloth and white vinegar mixed one to one, then rinse again.
3. Store the box away from sunlight.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

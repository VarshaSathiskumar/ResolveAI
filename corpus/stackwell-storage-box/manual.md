# Stackwell Clear Box 30L User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome

Thank you for choosing the Stackwell Clear Box 30L. The box holds up to 20 kg and stacks up to four high when all lids are clipped on.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Care

Wash the box with warm water and mild soap. Do not use bleach or put the box in a dishwasher. Keep it out of direct sunlight for long periods, which can make the plastic cloudy.

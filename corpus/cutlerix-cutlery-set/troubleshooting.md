# Cutlerix 24-piece Cutlery Set Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Rust spots | Salt or bleach left on the steel | Rust spots |
| Water marks | Hard water | Water marks |
| Bent tines | Misuse | Bent tines |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Rust spots

Small orange spots appear on the steel.

1. Rub the spot with a paste of baking soda and water using a soft cloth.
2. Rinse and dry the piece straight away.
3. Keep the cutlery away from bleach and salty water.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Water marks

White marks remain after washing.

1. Wipe the piece with a cloth dampened with white vinegar.
2. Rinse and dry by hand.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Bent tines

The tines of a fork are bent.

1. Bending them back can weaken the steel. Replace the piece if it is bent by more than a few millimetres.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

# Hydra Steel Bottle 750 User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome

Thank you for choosing the Hydra Steel Bottle 750. The vacuum insulation keeps drinks cold for 24 hours and hot for 12 hours.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Care

Wash by hand with warm water and a bottle brush. Do not put the bottle in a dishwasher or freezer. Do not fill it with carbonated drinks.

# Hydra Steel Bottle 750 Troubleshooting Guide

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Lid leaks | Seal ring out of place | Lid leaks |
| Bad smell or taste | Residue in the bottle | Odour |
| Drink does not stay cold | Damaged insulation | Insulation |

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Lid leaks

Water seeps out around the lid.

1. Unscrew the lid and pull out the seal ring.
2. Wash the ring and the groove, then press the ring back until it sits flat.
3. Screw the lid on straight and tighten it by hand.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Odour

The bottle smells or the water tastes odd.

1. Fill with warm water and a spoon of baking soda and leave overnight.
2. Rinse and let the bottle dry open.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Insulation

Drinks no longer stay cold or hot.

1. Check for a dent on the body, because a dent can break the vacuum.
2. If the outside of the bottle feels warm with a hot drink, contact support.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## When to contact support

If the problem does not go away after the steps above, contact support with the model and the steps you tried.

# Brewwell Espresso Studio ES-1 Limited Warranty

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Warranty term

The Espresso Studio ES-1 is covered by a 24 month limited warranty from the date of purchase. Keep your receipt.

## What is covered

Defects in materials and workmanship of the pump, boiler, control board and pressure gauge under normal household use.

## What is not covered

- Wear of the portafilter, baskets and group head gasket.
- Damage from limescale.
- Cosmetic damage, drops and misuse.
- Commercial use.

## Making a claim

Contact Brewwell support with your model, purchase date and the steps already tried. Support will open a case for covered faults.

# Brewwell Espresso Studio ES-1 Troubleshooting Guide

## Quick symptom table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| Pump buzzes loudly but no water comes out of the group head (E05) | Air lock in the pump | Pump airlock |
| Gauge climbs into the red and little or no espresso comes out | Grind too fine or basket overfilled | Choked shot |
| Weak, watery shot | Grind too coarse or old coffee | Weak shot |

## Pump airlock (error E05)

The pump buzzes or rattles, the machine will not brew and no water comes out of the group head even though the tank is full. Air has been trapped in the pump line, often after the tank ran dry or was removed while the pump was running.

1. Switch the machine off and refill the tank to the MAX line.
2. Remove the tank and check that its outlet valve is clean, then reseat it firmly.
3. Remove the portafilter. Place a large cup under the group head and open the steam wand over a second cup.
4. Switch on and run the brew switch for 10 seconds, then the steam switch for 10 seconds. Repeat this alternating pattern up to five times until water flows steadily from both.
5. Switch off, refit the portafilter and make a test shot.

If the pump still only buzzes after five alternating cycles, the pump may be faulty. This is covered by the warranty.

## Choked shot

Use a coarser grind and 18 g of coffee. Never force a locked portafilter. Release pressure by switching the steam switch on briefly.

## Weak shot

Use a finer grind and fresh beans. Check the tamp is level.

## Descaling

See the user manual, page 5. Descale every 2 months.

## When to contact support

Contact Brewwell support with the model, the error code or symptom and the steps already tried.

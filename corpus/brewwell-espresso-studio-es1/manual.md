# Brewwell Espresso Studio ES-1 User Manual

<!-- page: 1 -->
## Welcome and safety

The Espresso Studio ES-1 is a semi-automatic espresso machine with a 15 bar pump and a steam wand. The group head, portafilter and steam wand are hot during use. Keep the machine on a level surface.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Parts and controls

Removable 1.8 litre tank, portafilter with single and double baskets, group head, steam wand, pressure gauge, and three switches: power, brew and steam.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## First-time setup and priming

1. Fill the tank to the MAX line and seat it firmly.
2. Plug in and switch on. Wait for the ready light, about 40 seconds.
3. Prime the pump: place a cup under the group head with no portafilter fitted, switch the brew switch on and let water run until it flows steadily, then switch off.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Making espresso

1. Fill the basket with 18 g of ground coffee and tamp level.
2. Lock the portafilter in the group head.
3. Place a cup under it and switch the brew switch on. The gauge should rise to the 9 bar zone.
4. Stop after about 30 seconds or 36 g in the cup.

Use a medium-fine grind. Too fine a grind chokes the machine and the gauge sticks in the red zone.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## Cleaning and descaling

Backflush weekly with a blind basket and clean the portafilter. Descale every 2 months with Brewwell descaling solution. Empty and refill the tank after descaling and run the brew switch until the water runs clear.
